The approach

Controlled movement, not reps for the sake of reps.

Every session with Jade starts with how you move, not just what you lift. Alignment, breath and control come first — the strength follows.

Whether you're training privately, with a duet partner, or in a small group, the instruction stays close and specific to your body.
